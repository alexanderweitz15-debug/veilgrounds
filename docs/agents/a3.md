### Deviations from the prompt

- §9 rates "Iron ~8 s, Gold ~20 s, Diamond ~45 s, Challenger ~120 s" cannot produce the Table B shares with windows of
  0.5-3 s (max share = 3 s / interval: Gold 15 %, Challenger 2.5 %). As §9 says "Kalibriere Rate x Dauer", the
  share is binding: random distraction every ~5.7 s (Iron), ~9.4 s (Gold), ~18 s (Diamond), ~34 s (Challenger)
  start-to-start, durations median 1.6 s (Iron) .. 0.8 s (Challenger).
- Minimap glance (§9 "geplant, 0,3-0,8 s"): Challenger glances every 2 s (perception profile); counting every glance
  as distraction would be 15 %+ alone. "Geplant" is modelled as `minimapGlancePlannedPct` (Iron 0 .. Challenger 100):
  a planned glance falls into a free moment and costs nothing, an unplanned one opens a 0.3-0.8 s window.
- "Mein Fehler" is a team chat line until the ping kind exists (A3-8).
