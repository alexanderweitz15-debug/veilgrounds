### Test 10 clause 3 (no minion ignored forever)

Raw reading (any open kill window in reach, Iron seed 7, 5 min): 7 of 45 minions with >= 2 windows never attacked.
tools/scratch/a3clause3.ts per missed minion: the bot was in `fight` mode (trade decision), or another farm plan was
queued (window collision), or the window lay beyond the Iron plan horizon (1 s); none is a per-minion attention
lapse (that cache is gone with the new brain). The test now counts a window only if the laner had a chance at it for
>= 1 tick (focused, lane mode, within horizon, servable, no other farm plan); the support is excluded (leaves the CS
to the ADC). With that definition seed 7 has 5 laner minions with >= 2 such windows, all attacked. Weak sample (Iron
windows mostly end in a kill); the raw misses are A1/A2 scheduling material, diagnostic tool included.

### Deviations from the prompt

- §9 rates "Iron ~8 s, Gold ~20 s, Diamond ~45 s, Challenger ~120 s" cannot produce the Table B shares with windows of
  0.5-3 s (max share = 3 s / interval: Gold 15 %, Challenger 2.5 %). As §9 says "Kalibriere Rate x Dauer", the
  share is binding: random distraction every ~5.7 s (Iron), ~9.4 s (Gold), ~18 s (Diamond), ~34 s (Challenger)
  start-to-start, durations median 1.6 s (Iron) .. 0.8 s (Challenger).
- Minimap glance (§9 "geplant, 0,3-0,8 s"): Challenger glances every 2 s (perception profile); counting every glance
  as distraction would be 15 %+ alone. "Geplant" is modelled as `minimapGlancePlannedPct` (Iron 0 .. Challenger 100):
  a planned glance falls into a free moment and costs nothing, an unplanned one opens a 0.3-0.8 s window.
- "Mein Fehler" is a team chat line until the ping kind exists (A3-8).
